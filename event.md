ClawCamp Berlin Edition
​ClawCamp Berlin is a hands-on AI builder session bringing together people who want to actually build with AI rather than just talk about it.

​At this session, expect real workshops where you install, configure, and ship, not just slides and screenshots. Whether you are picking up new skills, working through a build challenge, or showing off something you made, the format is designed for participation.
